Please read the [agent guide](https://github.com/Arvex-GH/UnrealEngine-Proto-1/blob/main/AGENTS.md) and [bridge setup README](https://github.com/Arvex-GH/UnrealEngine-Proto-1/blob/main/bridge/README.md) to understand my local PC bridge; its [wait function](https://github.com/Arvex-GH/UnrealEngine-Proto-1/blob/main/bridge/wait.sql) is also on GitHub. Load the Supabase connector and use the `ArenaConnectMCP` project to submit authorized requests to the bridge queue, then call `arena_bridge_wait` with the returned job ID to retrieve results without waiting for me to report them. If a request remains pending or running, wait again within a reasonable deadline; don't resubmit it blindly. The worker may be running in auto mode, but only perform actions relevant to my request and confirm sensitive or irreversible operations first. If you can't access a link or connector, tell me what needs connecting rather than asking for credentials.
